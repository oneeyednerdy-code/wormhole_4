Privacy & storage

What Wormhole stores

Wormhole does not use advertising trackers or sell browser data. Information described as local stays in this browser and is not uploaded by Wormhole.

Essential storage

This is used without an optional-history choice because it is needed for requested features or accessibility:

  • Twitch OAuth state and the temporary Twitch access token used during the current browser session.
  • Your storage choice so Wormhole remembers whether optional history is allowed.
  • Your explicitly selected light/dark theme and high-contrast preferences.

Optional local history

If you choose Allow local history, Wormhole stores rolling viewer-count samples, observed channel/category snapshots, and up to five previous-stream references. These improve estimated averages, activity details, and offline matching.

Third-party services

Signing in and searching sends requests directly to Twitch. Twitch live-player content loads only after you choose a stream preview. Google Fonts provides the interface fonts. External Ko-fi and creator links open only when selected.

Your controls

Open Privacy settings in the Wormhole footer to change your choice or permanently delete locally stored viewer, channel, and previous-stream history.
