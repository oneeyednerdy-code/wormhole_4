Sponsor Wormhole

Wormhole helps Twitch streamers discover thoughtful raid destinations using live channel data, audience fit, tags, categories, and accessibility-focused controls. Sponsorship helps keep its core discovery features free.

Project sponsorship

Suitable for creator-focused services, accessibility technology, streaming tools, and community organizations whose work fits Wormhole’s audience.

Community support

Individuals can support development through Ko-fi. Support is appreciated, but it does not unlock required discovery features or change match rankings.

Possible placements

Clearly labeled footer recognition, a sponsor page listing, release-note acknowledgment, or a time-limited campaign placement can be discussed.

What is never sold

Sponsors do not receive Twitch tokens, local history, diagnostic logs, streamer identities, match rankings, or preferential placement in discovery results.

Standards and disclosure

Every paid placement will be labeled as sponsored. Wormhole does not accept sponsorships that require hidden tracking, misleading claims, discriminatory messaging, gambling promotion, or control over recommendation results. Final acceptance remains at the project owner’s discretion.

Request sponsorship information

Share your organization, campaign dates, proposed placement, budget range, and any required creative specifications. Audience figures or a media kit can be discussed using current, verifiable data rather than estimates placed on this public page.
