Privacy & storage

What Wormhole stores

Wormhole does not use advertising trackers or sell browser data. Information described as local stays in this browser and is not uploaded by Wormhole.

Essential storage

This is used without an optional-history choice because it is needed for requested features or accessibility:

  • Twitch OAuth state in browser storage or a short-lived SameSite cookie fallback, plus the temporary Twitch access token used during the current browser session.
  • Your storage choice so Wormhole remembers whether optional history is allowed.
  • Your explicitly selected light/dark theme, high-contrast preference, text size, relaxed spacing, reduced-motion choice, underlined-link choice, simplified-result preference, and manual device-layout override.
  • One filter preset only when you explicitly select Save current filters.

Optional local history

If you choose Allow local history, Wormhole stores rolling viewer-count samples, observed channel/category snapshots, up to five previous-stream references, and the latest 150 privacy-sanitized diagnostic events. These improve estimated averages, activity details, offline matching, and troubleshooting.

Third-party services

Signing in and searching sends requests directly to Twitch. Twitch live-player content loads after you choose a preview or after Twitch confirms a completed raid; the completed-raid view also loads Twitch's embedded chat. Wormhole does not automatically post raid-completed chat messages. Google Fonts provides the interface fonts. External Ko-fi and creator links open only when selected.

Your controls

Open Privacy settings in the Wormhole footer to change your choice or permanently delete locally stored viewer, channel, previous-stream, and diagnostic history. Diagnostics can also be cleared separately from the footer.
